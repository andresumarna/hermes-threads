Hermes Threads — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Hermes Threads is a private, single-user tool operated by Andre Sumarna ("I", "me"). It connects my own Threads account (@andresumarna) to a personal assistant that runs on my own computer, so I can draft, publish and measure my own posts and research public conversations about AI and software development. It is not offered to other people and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the app accesses

  • My own Threads account: profile information, my posts and replies, replies to my posts, mentions of my account, and insights (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, follower count).
  • Public Threads content: public posts returned by keyword or topic-tag search, and public profile information and posts of accounts I look up, used only to understand what is being discussed and to choose posts I may reply to.
  • No contact lists, private messages, payment data or data from people who have not posted publicly.

How the data is used

  • To draft posts and replies that I review and approve one by one before anything is published.
  • To publish my approved posts and replies, and to hide or unhide replies on my own posts.
  • To measure how my own posts perform and to summarise public discussion trends for my own content planning.

The data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, used to build profiles of individuals, or used to train machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored and for how long

  • The Threads access token is stored only on my own computer, in a private storage volume that only the app can read. It is never shared and is refreshed automatically before it expires.
  • Research notes and metric snapshots are stored on my own computer. Public post excerpts are kept for at most 90 days; my own account metrics are kept as long as I keep the project.
  • Text may be processed by the AI model provider configured for my assistant only to draft and summarise content for me; it is not used by the app for any other purpose.

Data deletion

To have any data about you that this app holds deleted, send a request to danremuranas@gmail.com with the subject "Hermes Threads data deletion" and your Threads username. I will delete any stored excerpts of your public posts and confirm within 30 days. You can also remove this app's access to your own account at any time in the Threads app: Settings → Account → Website permissions.
